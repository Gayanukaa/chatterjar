# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of good behavior: being kind and patient, respecting different opinions, giving
and accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, and
publishing other people's private information without their permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may ban contributors for
behavior they find inappropriate.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub private vulnerability
reporting (Security tab) or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports are reviewed
and kept confidential.
